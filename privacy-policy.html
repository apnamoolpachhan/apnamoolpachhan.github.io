At Apna Mool Pachhan (https://apnamoolpachhan.github.io), your privacy is very important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website, how we use it, and how we protect it.

By using our website, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only limited information:

  • Information you provide: When you fill our contact form or email us, you may provide your name, email address, phone number, village / pind name, and family history details.
  • Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we may collect your browser type, device type, pages visited, and time spent on our site through cookies or analytics tools.
  • We do NOT collect passwords, bank details, Aadhaar number, or any sensitive financial information.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only to:

  • Reply to your messages and help you find your ancestral village / family history
  • Improve our website content and user experience
  • Send you updates only if you ask us to
  • Keep our website safe and prevent spam / misuse

We will never sell your personal information to anyone.

3. Family History Information

Because our website is about family history (Bansawali), some users share names of their ancestors, village, and Gotra with us.

  • We will use this information only to help you, and only with your permission.
  • We will NOT publish your family tree, phone number, or personal details on our website without your clear permission.
  • You can ask us anytime to delete your information, and we will do it.

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure. Please do not send highly sensitive documents like passwords or bank details via email or contact form.

Our website is for general information and family history. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent. If you are a parent and think your child has shared information with us, please contact us and we will delete it.
