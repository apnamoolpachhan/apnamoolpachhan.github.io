At Apna Mool Pachhan, your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect and how we use and protect it.

1. Information We Collect

  • Information you provide: Name, email address, phone number, village or pind name, and family history details.

2. How We Use Your Information

Family history information is used only to provide requested help and is not published without clear permission. You may request correction or deletion at any time.

Other websites linked from this site have their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their content or practices.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission is completely secure. Please do not send passwords or highly sensitive documents.

7. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 without parental consent.

8. Your Rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, or stopping the use of your information by emailing us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy and will post the revised date on this page.
