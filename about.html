About Us

Welcome to Apna Mool Pachhan, an independent spiritual knowledge platform dedicated to sharing the timeless wisdom of Gurbani, Sikh philosophy, meditation and spirituality.

Apna Mool Pachhan means "Recognise Your True Self". Our aim is to inspire people to understand their inner nature, discover the deeper meaning of life and experience peace through spiritual awareness.

Our Mission

Our mission is to share meaningful spiritual knowledge and inspire people to develop peace, wisdom, kindness and self-awareness.

  • Share the timeless teachings of Gurbani.
  • Promote Waheguru Simran and Naam Simran.
  • Encourage meditation and self-awareness.
  • Share spiritual knowledge in simple language.
  • Inspire kindness, humility and compassion.

What We Share

  • Gurbani and Sikhism
  • Waheguru Simran and Naam Simran
  • Meditation and Samadhi
  • Spiritual Wisdom and Philosophy
  • Osho's Teachings
  • Self-Realisation and Awareness

Our Vision

Our vision is to make spiritual knowledge accessible and understandable to everyone. We hope to encourage a life based on peace, awareness, understanding, kindness and compassion.

Our Commitment

We aim to provide useful, informative and original content for our readers. We respect spiritual traditions and make continuous efforts to improve the quality and accuracy of our content.
