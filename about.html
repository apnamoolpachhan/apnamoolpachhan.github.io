ਆਪਣਾ ਮੂਲ ਪਛਾਣੋ - Know Your Roots

Connecting Punjabis Worldwide To Their Village, Their Family & Their Heritage

Who Are We?

Apna Mool Pachhan is a mission started from Punjab, India, with one simple dream — that no Punjabi should forget their roots.

After 1947 Partition and years of migration, lakhs of families moved from their pinds to cities and abroad — Canada, USA, UK, Australia and more. With time, many families lost connection with their ancestral village, their Jathera, their Gotra, and their family history. We are here to bring that connection back.

"ਜੋ ਆਪਣਾ ਮੂਲ ਭੁੱਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ, ਉਹ ਆਪਣੀ ਪਛਾਣ ਭੁੱਲ ਜਾਂਦਾ ਹੈ"
Whoever forgets his roots, forgets his identity.

Our Mission

  • Help people find their ancestral village (Pind Khoj)
  • Help families create their Bansawali / Family Tree
  • Preserve Sikh history, Jathera history, and Punjab's Virsa
  • Connect NRI families with their relatives in Punjab
  • Teach next generation about their culture and ancestors

What We Do

🏡 Pind Khoj

Find your ancestral pind, district, map and old history.

🌳 Bansawali

Make your family tree from your elders' memories and records.

📜 Itihas

History of your Gotra, Jathera, and your clan's contribution.

🤝 NRI Support

Help for NRIs who want to visit their pind or meet relatives.

Why This Matters?

Today many children in Canada, UK, USA do not know which pind their Dada-Dadi came from. They do not know their Gotra, their Jathera, or their family story.

We believe that if a child knows his roots, he will be proud of his identity. Our website is a small step to save this history before our elders leave us.
