About Us

Apna Mool Pachhan is an independent spiritual knowledge platform created to share valuable spiritual teachings and ideas with readers. Our website focuses on Gurbani, Sikh spirituality, Naam Simran, meditation, self-realisation and the wisdom of spiritual masters. Our intention is to make spiritual knowledge accessible, understandable and useful in daily life.

Our Mission

Our mission is to spread spiritual knowledge, awareness and positivity through authentic teachings and meaningful content.


We aim to:

Share the message of Gurbani.
Promote Naam Simran and meditation.
Encourage kindness, humility and compassion.
Explain spiritual concepts in simple language.
Inspire peace and self-awareness.
