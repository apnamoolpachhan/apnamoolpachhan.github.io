About Us

Apna Mool Pachhan is a mission to connect Punjabis across the world with their roots. Many families migrated after 1947, but their hearts still belong to their pind. We help you trace your family tree, find your ancestral village, Gotra, and Jathera history. Our aim is to preserve Punjab's Virsa for next generations.

Our Services

🏡 Pind Khoj

We help you find your ancestral village location, map, and history with old records.

🌳 Bansawali / Family Tree

We create your family tree / Bansawali based on family interviews and available records.

📜 Itihas & Virsa

Information about Sikh history, Jathera, Gotra, and cultural heritage of your clan.

🤝 NRI Help

Special help for NRIs who want to visit their pind or connect with relatives in Punjab.
